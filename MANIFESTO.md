# The SlowAI Manifesto

**Think first. AI when it matters.**

AI can create meaningful value. It also consumes resources, introduces risks and can add unnecessary complexity. SlowAI asks a question before choosing a model: **Do we need AI at all?**

## Our commitments
1. **Start with the problem.** Define the desired outcome and success criteria before choosing technology.
2. **Consider no-AI first.** Evaluate simpler software, deterministic rules, statistics, process improvements and human workflows.
3. **Use the minimum sufficient intelligence.** When AI adds value, prefer the least complex approach that meets quality, reliability and safety requirements.
4. **Measure the whole system.** Account for accuracy, latency, tokens, compute, energy, cost and—where evidence permits—environmental and lifecycle impacts.
5. **Protect people.** Evaluate privacy, fairness, accessibility, labor impacts, safety and accountability; keep human oversight where needed.
6. **Show the evidence.** Make methods, assumptions, baselines, limitations and uncertainty visible. Label simulations clearly.
7. **Build in the open.** Welcome constructive contributions and share reproducible tools and findings under appropriate open licenses.

## What SlowAI is not
SlowAI is not anti-AI, an endorsement of any provider, or a claim that smaller models always win. It is a disciplined way to make better decisions about **whether, when and how** to use AI.

## Our first experiment
The repository currently includes a synthetic industrial benchmark comparing agent-only reasoning with deterministic preprocessing and typed decision approaches. It is one demonstrator of the philosophy, not proof of broad environmental benefits.

**Less unnecessary AI. More meaningful value.**

What this experiment does not prove

This is fault-type classification on a published dataset, not AI Factory cooling-loop anomaly detection. Original annotation independence has not been independently verified. There is no meaningful expert-rule baseline yet, no frontier-model evaluation, and no measured energy, carbon or financial savings.

# SlowAI — Claude Code project instructions

SlowAI is an independent, community-driven open-source initiative. It is not an employer or corporate program. Keep company names, proprietary assets, internal acronyms, and confidential data out of public project materials unless independently authorized and properly licensed.

## Mission
Think first. AI when it matters. Compare non-AI alternatives before selecting the minimum sufficient AI; measure real-world benefits, cost, energy, environmental and societal impacts; publish reproducible evidence and limitations.

## Working principles
- Preserve existing benchmark code, history, and functionality. The current industrial benchmark is an initial demonstrator, not the entire mission.
- Prefer small, reviewable changes with clear tests and honest evidence.
- Never claim simulated benchmark results are measured provider performance.
- Do not introduce external services, telemetry, paid APIs, deployments, credentials, new dependencies, or publication of third-party material without explicit maintainer approval.
- Never commit secrets or personal data.
- Seek human review for changes with safety, privacy, legal, or operational implications.

## Pull request workflow
1. Branch from main (feature/, docs/, fix/, content/), commit, push, and open a PR describing changes, rationale, verification and risks.
2. The maintainer authorized Claude on 10 October 2026 (SlowAI handover brief) to merge its own PRs once all required checks pass. Never merge with failing or pending checks; request review for safety, privacy, legal or licensing changes.
3. Never bypass failing or pending CI, force-push main, or rewrite shared history.
4. Approval is required before deployments, changes to hosting or DNS, new external services or trackers, public release of third-party assets, or changes to licensing.
5. Before modifying or publishing benchmarks, check the provenance and publication rights of datasets, trademarks and model/provider results.

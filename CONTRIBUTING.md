# Contributing to SlowAI

Thank you for helping make AI decisions more evidence-based, efficient and responsible.

## Good first contributions
- Improve documentation, accessibility, tests or reproducibility.
- Propose a fair non-AI baseline or a smaller-model alternative.
- Add honest measurement methods and uncertainty reporting.
- Review privacy, sustainability and societal impact assumptions.

## How to contribute
1. Open an issue describing the problem, evidence and proposed change.
2. Fork the repository or create a topic branch.
3. Keep the change focused, add tests where applicable, and update relevant documentation.
4. Open a pull request explaining what changed, why, how it was verified and any trade-offs.
5. Wait for maintainer review before merging.

## Evidence and ethics
Use synthetic or properly licensed datasets; never submit confidential information, secrets or personal data. Distinguish simulation from real measurements, and disclose methodology, assumptions and conflicts of interest. Do not claim carbon or energy savings without defensible measurements and system boundaries.

# SlowAI Community Code of Conduct

SlowAI is a space for thoughtful, evidence-based and respectful collaboration.

## Our standards
- Be welcoming across backgrounds, experience levels and disciplines.
- Challenge ideas, claims and methodologies rather than attacking people.
- Disclose limitations, uncertainty, conflicts of interest and relevant affiliations.
- Respect privacy, consent, accessibility and intellectual property.
- Make room for disagreement: a credible non-AI baseline is as valuable as an AI result.

## Unacceptable behavior
Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, deliberate disruption and publication of another person's private information are not tolerated.

## Scope
This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces, and when representing SlowAI elsewhere.

## Reporting and enforcement
For now, report concerns privately to the repository maintainer through a suitable private GitHub contact channel rather than posting sensitive details in a public issue. If no private contact method is available, do not disclose private information publicly; the maintainer should establish a dedicated confidential reporting channel before inviting broad community participation.

The maintainer may moderate or remove content and restrict participation, taking context and severity into account. Reports should be handled discreetly and without retaliation.

This is an initial community policy; feedback and improvements are welcome.
